# Project research requirements

## User preference recorded 2026-10-14

Every proposed research question must have the most direct possible fit to WVAQ: Image/Video/Audio Quality Assessment in Computer Vision, VLM and Diffusion Model.

- Make image, video, or audio quality assessment central to the scientific question, experimental design, and proposed contribution.
- Explain the concrete quality-assessment problem, practical use case, and falsifiable initial hypothesis before proposing full-paper work.
- Evaluate candidate ideas for direct WVAQ fit first. Generic agent reliability, hallucination, or tool-selection ideas need a substantive quality-assessment contribution; adding WVAQ terminology is insufficient.
- Agentic systems may supply a use case, but must not displace the quality-assessment research objective.
- Distinguish motivation, novelty hypotheses, pilot evidence, and validated findings. Do not promise positive results or paper readiness before appropriate experiments.

The completed CPU pilot and its scoped negative decision remain documented in FINAL_CPU_RESEARCH_DECISION.md. This preference does not change those results or approve a replacement hypothesis.
